The 2021 Jeep Compass 80th Anniversary 4x4 in striking black is the ultimate choice for those who desire rugged dependability with refined handling. Perfectly suited for both city streets and off-the-beaten-path adventures, its 4x4 drivetrain ensures you're ready for any terrain.

The Compass stands out with its distinctive Neutral Grey exterior accents and Badging, complemented by sleek alloy wheels and eye-catching black grille with Neutral Grey Rings. The sophisticated black exterior is matched flawlessly with a luxurious black interior featuring Light Tungsten Accent Stitching, a Leather Steering Wheel, and Berber Floor Mats adorned with the 80th Anniversary Tag.

This special edition is packed with advanced technology, including an 8.4” Touchscreen, GPS Navigation, and Smart Device Integration. Safety is a priority with features like a backup camera, brake assist, heated mirrors, and a 7” Customizable In-Cluster Display. Comfort is guaranteed with heated seats and a heated steering wheel—perfect for cold Canadian winters.

Ideal for adventurous families and tech-savvy individuals alike, this Jeep Compass offers ample space and convenience with its Power Windows, Keyless Entry, and Remote Start system.
